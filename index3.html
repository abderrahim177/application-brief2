The Yellow River, or "Huang He." is not just a river, it's a living testament to China's rich history and cultural heritage. Spanning over 5,400 kilometers My journey along this iconic river offered a perfect blend of natural wonders, cultural experiences, and awe-inspiring moments From the serene alpine meadows at its source in Qinghai Province to the thunderous Hukou Waterfall, and finally, to its peaceful estuary meeting the Bohai Sea, every stop revealed. a unique story. The golden cliffs of the Loess Plateau showcased nature's artistry, while the bustling city of Lanzhou introduced me to delicious local cuisine and vibrant urban life.
